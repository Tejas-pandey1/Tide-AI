Questions people ask

Does it hurt?

We numb properly and go at your pace. Raise a hand and we stop. Most patients say it was easier than they feared.

Can I be seen today if I’m in pain?

We keep emergency slots open every day. Call before 4 PM and we’ll do our best to see you the same day.

Do you accept insurance?

Yes, with most major insurers. We help with the claim paperwork. Bring your card to the first visit.

Can I pay in instalments?

For treatments above ₹10,000 we offer 3 and 6 month plans at no extra charge.
